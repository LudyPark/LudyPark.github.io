PAYMENTS

Making payment outcomes safe when gateways are asynchronous.

I have designed payment workflows around explicit state, idempotent request handling, callback processing, and recovery paths rather than assuming a single synchronous success path.

  • Separate the customer-facing workflow from the provider transaction state.
  • Treat retries, dead-letter handling, and idempotency as distinct reliability tools.
  • Use an authoritative inquiry path when the provider outcome is unresolved.

MOBILITY · IOT

Scaling message processing without losing ownership.

I have worked on distributed Kotlin/Spring systems for mobility and connected-device workflows, with a focus on clear boundaries between inbound events, command delivery, and persistent connections.

  • Use broker-mediated distribution to scale independent consumer work.
  • Route commands to the service instance that owns a persistent device connection.
  • Plan for reconnects, duplicate delivery, lag, and observable recovery.

RELIABILITY

Tracing operational symptoms to the actual bottleneck.

I investigate production symptoms by following measurable behavior before choosing a fix: active work, transaction boundaries, constrained resources, and the external dependencies that shape recovery.

  • Use runtime evidence to distinguish active work from retained-memory hypotheses.
  • Keep database transactions short around slow external dependencies.
  • Validate operational and cost improvements over time rather than trusting one observation.
